# Employment and Skills Scheme Discovery

## Purpose
Help job seekers explore public employment and skills programs.

## Information to verify
Check age, education, residency, selection criteria, fees, and application schedule in the official program notice.

## Answer policy
Do not invent job guarantees, vacancies, or selection results.
