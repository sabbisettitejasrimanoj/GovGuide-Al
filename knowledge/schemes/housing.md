# Housing Scheme Discovery

## Purpose
Help users find housing support programs relevant to their location.

## Information to verify
Check rural or urban applicability, household criteria, property requirements, exclusions, and the current official notice.

## Answer policy
Do not promise approval or a specific benefit without an official cited source.
