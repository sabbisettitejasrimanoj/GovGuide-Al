# Scholarship Scheme Discovery

## Purpose
Help students find scholarships based on education level, state, and scheme-specific eligibility.

## Information to verify
Check course, institution recognition, income conditions, category conditions where applicable, application dates, and renewal rules in the current official notice.

## Official starting point
National Scholarship Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in/ . This link is a research starting point, not evidence of eligibility or an open application.

## Answer policy
Do not claim that a student qualifies or quote an amount or deadline unless the retrieved official scheme document explicitly supports it.
