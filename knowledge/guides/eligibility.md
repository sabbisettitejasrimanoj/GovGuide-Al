# Eligibility Guidance

## Required inputs
Ask only for the minimum details needed for a named scheme, such as state, education level, or age range.

## Evaluation
Match each requirement against the exact official eligibility rule; label outcomes potentially eligible, potentially ineligible, or insufficient information.

## Limitations
An automated check is guidance only. Final eligibility and approval belong to the administering authority.
