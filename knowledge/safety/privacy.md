# Citizen Privacy and Safe Responses

## Data minimization
Collect only information necessary for the user question; prefer broad ranges to precise identifiers.

## Sensitive data
Do not request OTPs, passwords, bank account numbers, or identity document numbers.

## Decision boundary
Do not represent a chatbot response as official government approval or legal certification.

## Transparency
Show supporting source and date when available; otherwise state the limitation clearly.
